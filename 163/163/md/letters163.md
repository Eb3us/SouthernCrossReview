# Letters to the Editor SCR 163

[The Christian Mystery -- Lecture 2 ](https://southerncrossreview.org/163/steiner-christian-mystery-2.html)

Hi Southern Cross Review,

Steiner, not Christ, is pretty much a mystery to me. In \"Just as there
has to be a mineral world so that the plant world may exist, so did the
apostles have to exist for the Christ Jesus to be there," he seems to be
citing, without acknowledging it, Jewish thinking, right? Based on the
OT. What nonsense. Jesus Christ grew the apostles, one by one, as is
clear from the NT. Only on that basis does this quote make perfect
sense: \"The servant is no greater than his lord." Steiner is a good
example of the German tendency of over-complicating things and be
mystified by that activity, an irreversible cycle leading to ultimate
obfuscation of what the point might have been. To me, what can't be said
with clarity seems not worth saying at all.

Nick Matsumoto
