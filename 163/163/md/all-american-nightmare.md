# An All-American Nightmare

### How to Build a Deportation Machine in the Age of Trump

## By [Michael Gould-Wartofsky](https://tomdispatch.com/authors/michaelgouldwartofsky/)

"Flights to Guantánamo Bay have begun. The worst of the worst have no
place in our homeland."

With those words the U.S. government
[announced](https://x.com/CBP/status/1886936769242845237?mx=2) the fate
awaiting "criminal aliens" in its custody.

On a military base in El Paso, Texas, masked men in combat fatigues
paraded a group of young Venezuelan immigrants, their hands cuffed and
their ankles shackled, in front of the cameras, before loading them onto
a waiting Air Force C-17, which was to deliver its human cargo to [Naval
Station Guantánamo
Bay](https://www.defense.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/Article/4055497/first-flight-of-illegal-aliens-arrives-at-guantanamo/)
overnight.

Once there, they were to be incarcerated in the infamous Camp 6,
[held](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/02/05/us/politics/migrants-trump-guantanamo-prison.html)
incommunicado in the same cells where al-Qaeda suspects were once held
in indefinite detention, and
[guarded](https://www.militarytimes.com/news/your-military/2025/02/03/troops-arrive-at-guantanamo-bay-to-prepare-migrant-detention-center/)
by the 1st Battalion, 6th Marine Regiment. Meanwhile, a [tent
city](https://www.washingtonpost.com/immigration/2025/02/04/guantanamo-bay-immigrants-deportation-flights/),
which could ultimately house as many as
[30,000](https://apnews.com/article/trump-signs-laken-riley-act-immigration-crackdown-30a34248fa984d8d46b809c3e6d8731a)
detainees, rises around the prison.

Though most of those immigrants have since been returned to Venezuela,
the Pentagon has
[pledged](https://www.defense.gov/News/Advisories/Advisory/Article/4076184/secretary-of-defense-pete-hegseth-to-travel-to-guantanamo-bay/)
to continue using the base for the "temporary detention of illegal
aliens who are pending return."

Back on the mainland, the Department of Defense (DoD) is
[deploying](https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/4037500/acting-secretary-of-defense-robert-salesses-statement-on-dod-actions-responding/)
thousands of troops to "seal the borders"; the Department of Justice
(DoJ) is
[deputizing](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2025/01/23/statement-dhs-spokesperson-directive-expanding-immigration-law-enforcement)
its agents to round up undocumented immigrants; Immigration and Customs
Enforcement (ICE) is
[mobilizing](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/national-security/ice-conduct-major-immigration-operations-three-cities-week-rcna189608)
to meet its daily quota of 1,200 to 1,500 arrests, armed with [target
lists](https://www.washingtonpost.com/immigration/2025/02/15/ice-arrests-immigration-deportations/),
[surveillance
technology](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/01/25/technology/trump-immigration-deportation-surveillance.html),
and ["less-lethal"
weapons](https://www.azcentral.com/story/news/politics/immigration/2025/02/03/phoenix-ice-arrest-61-year-old-man-involves-armored-vehicle-stun-grenades/78189148007/);
and immigration detention facilities [are to be
built](https://www.npr.org/2025/02/24/nx-s1-5307156/immigration-dhs-memo-trump-migrant-detention-military-bases)
on military bases across the country.

And that's not all either. Entire families are set to be
[detained](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/national-security/trump-administration-preparing-restart-immigrant-family-detention-rcna191230),
and the grim family-separation policy of the first Trump administration
[revived](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2024/dec/26/trump-family-detention-immigration-tom-homan).
Humanitarian parole is to be
[revoked](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2025/01/21/statement-dhs-spokesperson-directives-expanding-law-enforcement-and-ending-abuse),
refugees
[rejected](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/realigning-the-united-states-refugee-admissions-program/),
and asylum seekers
[returned](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/securing-our-borders/).
And cities, counties, and states that dare to defy the deportation
regime are to be
[punished](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/protecting-the-american-people-against-invasion/).

[Buy the Book](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0199313911/ref=nosim/?tag=tomdispatch-20)

The machinery of mass deportation has been set in motion in a
nightmarish fashion. It is meant to be impossible to stop --- or at
least to appear that way. Still,
[history](https://www.haymarketbooks.org/books/1086-no-one-is-illegal-updated-edition)
teaches us that such a machine, like any other, can be brought to a
halt, if only we understand how the apparatus actually works.

Here, then, is a simple, step-by-step guide to how the Trump
administration plans to build the machinery necessary to "complete
the [largest deportation
operation](https://apnews.com/article/trump-speech-congress-transcript-751b5891a3265ff1e5c1409c391fef7c) in
American history."

**1. Declare a state of emergency.**

"Today, I will sign a series of historic executive orders," Trump
[pledged](https://www.whitehouse.gov/remarks/2025/01/the-inaugural-address/)
in his Inaugural Address. "With these actions, we will begin the
complete restoration of America and the revolution of common sense."

That "revolution" in immigration enforcement did indeed begin with a
[barrage](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions) of such
orders, many lifted directly from the [Project
2025](https://static.project2025.org/2025_MandateForLeadership_CHAPTER-05.pdf)
[playbook](https://static.project2025.org/2025_MandateForLeadership_CHAPTER-04.pdf).

First among them was the declaration of a [state of
emergency](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/declaring-a-national-emergency-at-the-southern-border-of-the-united-states/)
in this country's borderlands. According to the National Emergencies Act
of
[1976](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/COMPS-10385/pdf/COMPS-10385.pdf),
this allows the military to be called up for domestic duties, whether to
the southern border, Guantánamo Bay, or anywhere else the president sees
fit.

**2. Equate immigration with an "invasion."**

"I have determined that the current situation at the southern border
qualifies as an invasion," reads another
[order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/guaranteeing-the-states-protection-against-invasion/)
signed on January 20th, citing [Article
IV](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/constitution-transcript) of
the Constitution.

"Accordingly," the order
[continues](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/guaranteeing-the-states-protection-against-invasion/),
"I hereby suspend the physical entry of any alien engaged in the
invasion." It goes on to authorize operations to "repel, repatriate, or
remove" non-citizens.

This is the logical conclusion of years of far-right propaganda about a
"[Third
World](https://archive.org/details/stateofemergency00buch/page/n5/mode/2up),"
"[Hispanic](https://www.nytimes.com/2019/08/03/us/patrick-crusius-el-paso-shooter-manifesto.html),"
or "[alien](https://biggs.house.gov/alien-invasion-documentary)"
"[invasion](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2019/08/11/business/media/el-paso-killer-conservative-media.html)"
of the United States, which, over time, has spread from the stuff of
[8chan
manifestos](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2019/aug/03/el-paso-shooting-21-year-old-suspect-in-custody-as-officials-investigate-possible-hate)
to the preambles of presidential proclamations.

**3. Expand Immigration and Customs Enforcement**.

The architecture of ICE is slated to
[expand](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2025/feb/21/trump-senate-budget-deportation-taxes)
to levels not seen since its founding in 2003.

The agency reportedly made more than
[14,000](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/trump-set-broaden-arrests-deportation-routes-expand-immigration-crackdown-2025-02-21/)
arrests in the first three weeks of Trump's second term. With it still
supposedly [failing](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/rcna191273) to
meet its quotas, however, officials want to
[double](https://www.npr.org/2025/01/16/nx-s1-5262921/laken-riley-act-deportation-ice)
the size of the force.

Now, Senate Republicans are proposing no less than [\$175
billion](https://apnews.com/article/senate-budget-trump-tax-cuts-deportations-48f6565ccb0fd6002734dbb5c3c3ffb7)
in new spending on immigration enforcement, while the House GOP is
looking to fund that spending spree with billions of dollars in
[cuts](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/looking-to-slash-medicaid-funding-republicans-consider-cutting-billions-of-dollars)
to Medicaid and other essential social services.

**4. Draft the Department of Justice.**

ICE is no longer to bear its burden alone. Since Trump's inauguration,
the DoJ, including the U.S. Attorney's Offices, the Federal Bureau of
Investigation (FBI), and the Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA), have
been
[pressed](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2025/01/23/statement-dhs-spokesperson-directive-expanding-immigration-law-enforcement)
into the service of the deportation machine.

The FBI, for instance, has been
[tasked](https://www.documentcloud.org/documents/25501154-doj-all-staff-memo-jan-21/)
with finding "identifying information and/or biometric data relating to
non-citizens located illegally in the US" --- data that will fuel the
detention-to-deportation pipeline.

"We've got special agents, intelligence analysts, and more, supporting
DHS \[Department of Homeland Security\] teams across the country,"
[said](https://www.fbi.gov/news/speeches/acting-director-brian-driscolls-statement-on-recent-fbi-achievements)
then-Acting FBI Director Brian Driscoll, "from New York and Chicago to
El Paso, Newark, and Denver."

**5. Deputize local law enforcement.**

ICE has also [partnered](https://www.ice.gov/identify-and-arrest/287g)
with local police departments, county sheriff's offices, and departments
of correction through a program known as
[287(g)](https://www.americanimmigrationcouncil.org/research/287g-program-immigration)
to "identify and remove incarcerated criminal aliens" before they can be
freed.

In New York City, Mayor [Eric
Adams](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/ng-interactive/2025/feb/21/eric-adams-trump-immigrants-rikers)
has typically promised to reopen an ICE office on [Rikers
Island](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2025/feb/13/nyc-eric-adams-rikers-ice),
purportedly as part of a [quid pro
quo](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/02/11/us/politics/trump-orders-bribery-corruption.html)
with the Trump administration.

And in February, Florida became the first state to
[sign](https://www.flgov.com/eog/news/press/2025/governor-ron-desantis-announces-additional-memoranda-agreement-between-florida-law)
a statewide 287(g) agreement, which would train officers of the Florida
Highway Patrol and State Guard to "interrogate any suspected alien or
person believed to be an alien."

**6. Criminalize all immigrants as "criminal aliens."**

When White House Press Secretary Katherine Leavitt was asked how many of
those arrested since January 20th had a criminal record and how many
were "just in the country illegally," she
[replied](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2025/01/press-briefing-by-press-secretary-karoline-leavitt/https:/www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2025/01/press-briefing-by-press-secretary-karoline-leavitt/),
"All of them. Because they illegally broke our nation's laws, and
therefore, they are criminals."

Tellingly, [fewer than
half](https://www.propublica.org/article/donald-trump-immigration-executive-orders)
of the 8,200 people arrested in the first two weeks of the Trump
administration had criminal convictions of any kind. And of the
approximately 4,400 detained in the first two weeks of February, more
than
[1,800](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/national-security/new-ice-data-shows-administration-isnt-just-arresting-criminals-rcna192656)
had never been charged with a crime.

**7. Hunt for "criminal aliens" everywhere**.

"Police, open the door! *Policía, abra la puerta*!"

Those words echoed across a Denver apartment complex, as [ICE
agents](https://coloradosun.com/2025/02/05/ice-raids-aurora-denver/)
with long guns backed by [BearCat](https://www.lencoarmor.com/) tactical
vehicles went door-to-door, asking residents for identification.
Twenty-nine members of the Cedar Run community were [rounded
up](https://abcnews.go.com/US/denver-ice-raids-targeting-100-gang-members-yielded/story?id=118574573)
in one go.

But ICE and its partners are not just hunting for undocumented
immigrants in their homes. Thanks to a [rule
change](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2025/01/21/statement-dhs-spokesperson-directives-expanding-law-enforcement-and-ending-abuse)
instituted by DHS, federal agents are also pursuing their prey in
locations previously deemed too
"[sensitive](https://www.ice.gov/doclib/ero-outreach/pdf/10029.2-policy.pdfhttps:/www.ice.gov/doclib/ero-outreach/pdf/10029.2-policy.pdf)"
for immigration enforcement purposes like
[schools](https://stateline.org/2025/02/03/state-local-officials-plan-for-potential-immigration-enforcement-at-schools/),
[hospitals](https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/hospitals-protected-areas-immigration-enforcement-trump/738102/),
[courtrooms](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2025/feb/18/trump-immigration-dragnet),
and
[churches](https://www.cnn.com/2025/01/27/politics/immigration-raids-federal-agents-uniform/index.html)
(though a federal judge in Maryland has [already
forbidden](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-judge-immigration-arrests-places-of-worship-quakers-baptists-sikhs/)
the Trump administration from carrying out such actions in certain
houses of worship).

**8. Collect biometric data.**

Another of Trump's executive orders
[announced](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/securing-our-borders/)
his intention to reauthorize the DoJ and DHS to collect DNA samples from
all detained "non-United States persons."

This DNA collection program is just one part of a vast surveillance
apparatus that has been built up over the years, which now requires vast
troves of [biometric](https://www.dhs.gov/biometrics) and biographic
data to be collected, stored, and analyzed.

Increasingly, that task has fallen to [for-profit
firms](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/expect-shift-in-us-border-strategy-to-pay-dividends-for-biometrics-surveillance-tech-providers).
Since 2020, the federal government has spent an estimated [\$7.8
billion](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/01/25/technology/trump-immigration-deportation-surveillance.html)
on such surveillance technologies, including a \$96 million contract
with Peter Thiel's data-mining firm
[Palantir](https://www.motherjones.com/politics/2025/02/palantir-alex-karp-trump-private-prisons-profiteers/).

**9. Put immigrants in prisons.**

The most recent
[data](https://tracreports.org/whatsnew/email.250218.html) shows that
America's immigrant detention centers are already over capacity, with
41,500 beds and 43,759 inmates. ICE is now seeking to more than
[triple](https://www.npr.org/2025/01/16/nx-s1-5262921/laken-riley-act-deportation-ice)
that capacity.

Trump
[pledged](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/protecting-the-american-people-against-invasion/),
on Day One, that he would allocate "all legally available resources" to
immigrant detention, evidently including America's
[prisons](https://abcnews.go.com/amp/US/males-detained-ice-housed-federal-prisons-new-memo/story?id=118850412).
In February, the Federal Bureau of Prisons [took
in](https://www.govexec.com/management/2025/02/federal-prisons-house-ice-detainees-trump-furthers-immigration-crackdown/402850/)
the first ICE detainees at facilities in Atlanta, Los Angeles, Miami,
Philadelphia, and elsewhere.

The policy also embraces military bases. The [Northern
Command](https://www.cbsnews.com/colorado/news/colorado-military-provide-facilities-process-detained-migrants)
is currently "providing facilities at Buckley Space Force Base... to
enable \[ICE\] to stage and process criminal aliens within the US."

**10. Privatize immigrant detention.**

More than 90% of such detainees are already
[overseen](https://wp.api.aclu.org/news/immigrants-rights/unchecked-growth-private-prison-corporations-and-immigration-detention-three-years-into-the-biden-administration)
by private contractors. Now, ICE is planning to warehouse thousands more
by leasing [mobile
structures](https://www.semafor.com/article/02/06/2025/trump-administration-container-company-to-provide-temporary-migrant-housing)
from a shipping container company.

And a new
[plan](https://www.politico.com/news/2025/02/25/documents-military-contractors-mass-deportations-022648),
floated by former Blackwater CEO [Erik
Prince](https://theintercept.com/2024/02/10/erik-prince-off-leash-imperialism-colonialism/),
would sell the government "outside assistance" in the form of privatized
"processing camps," along with a "small army" of private citizens with
the power to arrest and detain immigrants.

For the prison industry, the deportation drive has proven to be a
profitable enterprise indeed. "This is truly one of the most exciting
periods in my career," said CoreCivic's CEO on a recent
[call](https://theintercept.com/2025/02/11/corecivic-private-prison-trump-immigrant-detention/)
with investors.

**11. Bring back family detention.**

During the first Trump administration, America was haunted by the
specter of immigrant children in cages. Now, the architect of the
president's "zero-tolerance" policy and recently appointed "border czar"
[Tom
Homan](https://www.washingtonpost.com/immigration/2024/12/26/immigration-border-tom-homan-trump/)
plans to
[revive](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/national-security/trump-administration-preparing-restart-immigrant-family-detention-rcna191230)
family detention on a whole new scale.

Family detention centers, according to the [*Detention Watch
Network*](https://www.detentionwatchnetwork.org/pressroom/releases/2025/return-family-detention-unconscionable),
have a "well-documented history of negligence and abuse." Despite that
sordid history, ICE is reportedly
[readying](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/national-security/trump-administration-preparing-restart-immigrant-family-detention-rcna191230)
a "Request for Proposal" (RFP) for "detention facilities intended
specifically for families."

At the same time, the administration is [making it
harder](https://acf.gov/sites/default/files/documents/orr/ORR-FG-26-Revised-Fingerprint-Requirements-for-Sponsors-and-HHM--02-14-2025-.pdf)
for sponsors of immigrant children to free them from detention.

**12. Send asylum seekers to other countries.**

The deportation machine is no longer simply an American enterprise. It
is now an international affair, with [Costa
Rica](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/americas/costa-rica-donald-trump-panama-b2702440.html),
[El
Salvador](https://apnews.com/article/migration-rubio-panama-colombia-venezuela-237f06b7d4bdd9ff1396baf9c45a2c0bhttps:/apnews.com/article/migration-rubio-panama-colombia-venezuela-237f06b7d4bdd9ff1396baf9c45a2c0b),
[Guatemala](https://www.npr.org/2025/02/06/nx-s1-5288663/guatemala-us-deportation-rubio),
[Honduras](https://apnews.com/article/costa-rica-panama-mass-deportations-trump-migration-detention-85e70a0e4e053ea01b77b0e4d55c8fea),
and
[Panama](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/02/18/world/americas/trump-migrant-deportation-panama.html)
already taking in thousands of "third-country deportees."

"We have offered the USA the opportunity to outsource part of its prison
system," says El Salvadoran President [Nayib
Bukele](https://x.com/nayibbukele/status/1886606794614587573), whose
prisons are rife with [human rights
violations](https://www.amnesty.org/en/documents/amr29/7423/2023/en/).

In [Panama](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c3rndygqll7o), hundreds of
deportees of Central and East Asian origin were recently locked in a
hotel, then relocated to a makeshift camp in the middle of the jungle.
"It looks like a zoo, there are fenced cages,"
[according](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/02/19/world/americas/us-migrants-panama-jungle-camp.html)
to one eyewitness.

**13. Designate gangs as "foreign terrorist organizations."**

One of the president's most egregious orders
[asserts](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/designating-cartels-and-other-organizations-as-foreign-terrorist-organizations-and-specially-designated-global-terrorists/)
that alleged gang affiliations are sufficient to warrant a "terrorist"
designation.

Declaring it "time for America to [wage
war](https://apnews.com/article/trump-speech-congress-transcript-751b5891a3265ff1e5c1409c391fef7c) on
the cartels," Trump has specifically
[targeted](https://www.npr.org/2025/01/24/g-s1-44644/trump-drug-cartels)
Mexican, Central American, and Venezuelan nationals suspected of having
ties to the drug cartels, Mara Salvatrucha (MS-13), or the Tren de
Aragua gang, seeking their "expedited removal" or their "total
elimination."

The same order signals the president's intention to invoke the [Alien
Enemies
Act](https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/research-reports/alien-enemies-act-explained),
a wartime law dating to
[1798](https://maint.loc.gov/law/help/statutes-at-large/5th-congress/session-2/c5s2ch66.pdf),
which would subject "all natives, citizens, denizens, or subjects" of a
"hostile" nation to being "apprehended, restrained, secured and removed,
as alien enemies."

**14. Deport international students and workers who protest U.S.
policy.**

"To all the resident aliens who joined in the pro-jihadist protests, we
put you on notice: come 2025, we will find you, and we will deport you."
So warned the
[statement](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/01/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-takes-forceful-and-unprecedented-steps-to-combat-anti-semitism/)
accompanying the president's January 29th [executive
order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/additional-measures-to-combat-anti-semitism/),
which singled out supposedly "pro-Hamas aliens and left-wing radicals"
in higher education for "removal."

Authorities have evidently already begun implementing that order, with
[reports](https://www.northjersey.com/story/news/2025/02/13/us-revoking-student-visas-pro-palestinian-protesters/78387111007/)
of Arab students facing deportation for participating in pro-Palestine
protests. Over the weekend, ICE agents showed up at the door of Mahmoud
Khalil, a
Palestinian [activist](https://actionnetwork.org/letters/demand-the-immediate-release-of-columbia-student-pro-palestine-advocate-mahmoud-khalil-from-dhs-detention) at
Columbia University whose green card had reportedly been revoked by the
Trump administration. While in government custody, Khalil
was [disappeared](https://apnews.com/article/columbia-university-mahmoud-khalil-ice-15014bcbb921f21a9f704d5acdcae7a8) for
several days.

"I've seen enough," says Abed Ayoub, executive director of the
American-Arab Anti-Discrimination Committee, "to know that targeting is
happening."

**15. Freeze refugee admissions.**

"Refugee arrivals to the United States have been suspended until further
notice." That was the message on January 21st from the Office of Refugee
Resettlement, now under the
[leadership](https://www.propublica.org/article/immigration-children-ice-office-refugee-resettlement-mellissa-harper)
of a senior ICE official. With a stroke of the pen, President Trump has
[frozen](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/realigning-the-united-states-refugee-admissions-program/)
America's Refugee Admissions Program.

In so doing, he has left at least
[10,000](https://apnews.com/article/refugees-flights-trump-immigration-border-resettlement-33ebaa34bc4d0c069a22ee7aa5f8ff6d)
refugees in legal limbo, while abandoning hundreds of thousands more to
their fates in places like
[Afghanistan](https://www.hrw.org/asia/afghanistan), the
[Congo](https://www.hrw.org/africa/democratic-republic-congo), and
[Myanmar](https://www.hrw.org/asia/myanmar-burma).

Ultimately, the president would make one exception to the rule --- for
[white South
Africans](https://www.theatlantic.com/ideas/archive/2025/02/trump-south-africa-resettlement/681651/).
An [executive
order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/02/addressing-egregious-actions-of-the-republic-of-south-africa/)
signed on February 7th would "promote the resettlement of Afrikaner
refugees" as a protected class.

**16. Terminate Temporary Protected Status.**

Under the new administration's
[policies](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2025/01/21/statement-dhs-spokesperson-directives-expanding-law-enforcement-and-ending-abuse),
hundreds of thousands of Cubans, Haitians, Nicaraguans, Ukrainians, and
Venezuelans, among others, are set to lose their [Temporary Protected
Status](https://www.americanimmigrationcouncil.org/research/temporary-protected-status-overview)
(TPS) --- a form of humanitarian parole that permitted asylum seekers
from those countries to continue living and working in the U.S.

Citizenship and Immigration Services has announced an "[administrative
pause](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/u-s-pauses-immigration-applications-for-certain-migrants-welcomed-under-biden/)"
on all pending parole requests, while DHS,
[claiming](https://www.dhs.gov/sites/default/files/2025-01/25_0123_er-and-parole-guidance.pdf)
parole is a right "to which no alien is entitled," has authorized its
agents to strip immigrants of such protections.

ICE agents have already started making
[arrests](https://www.reuters.com/investigates/special-report/usa-trump-migration-ice/)
of TPS holders in Texas.

**17. Undermine the principle of birthright citizenship.**

Of all the president's orders, the most consequential for citizens is
the one that would
[rescind](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/protecting-the-meaning-and-value-of-american-citizenship/)
birthright citizenship, which would deny the rights guaranteed by the
[Fourteenth
Amendment](https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/14th-amendment)
to "persons born or naturalized in the U.S."

In practice, it would mean stripping citizenship rights from children
born here to mothers who are "unlawfully present" or whose presence is
"lawful but temporary."

For now, the order has been blocked by a Seattle judge's
[injunction](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.wawd.343943/gov.uscourts.wawd.343943.114.0_4.pdf),
but it will undoubtedly fall to the Supreme Court to decide its fate
(and the fate of the Constitution of which it's a part).

**18. Investigate citizens who would defend immigrant rights.**

As it happens, immigrants and their American-born children are not the
only ones in the crosshairs. Federal agents are now actively
[soliciting](https://www.documentcloud.org/documents/25517962-statement-of-objectives/)
bids for "Internet based threat risk mitigation and monitoring services"
in order to
[surveil](https://theintercept.com/2025/02/11/ice-immigration-social-media-surveillance/)
suspected political enemies on social media.

That initiative is part of what could become a coast-to-coast crackdown.
The Federal Communications Commission (FCC) has, ominously enough,
launched a "[formal
investigation](https://www.foxnews.com/media/fcc-chair-says-its-really-concerning-soros-backed-radio-station-exposed-undercover-ice-agents)"
into a local radio station, the San Francisco-based KCBS 740 AM, for
[reporting](https://www.kqed.org/news/12025977/fcc-investigates-sf-radio-station-for-ice-reporting-sparking-press-freedom-fears)
on the whereabouts of ICE agents.

And only recently, Tom Homan, designated the "border czar" by President
Trump, invited the Department of Justice to
[investigate](https://www.rollingstone.com/politics/politics-news/border-czar-prosecute-aoc-immigrants-rights-1235269988/)
Representative Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez (D-NY), reportedly in
retaliation for a "Know Your Rights" training session held under the
auspices of her office.

**19. Punish "sanctuary" cities, counties, and states.**

On Day One of the president's second term, the White House
[announced](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/protecting-the-american-people-against-invasion/)
that it was going on the warpath against "sanctuary" jurisdictions,
where local laws place limits on the involvement of law enforcement in
the business of immigration.

Since then, the Sanctuary Cities Enforcement [Working
Group](https://www.documentcloud.org/documents/25501154-doj-all-staff-memo-jan-21/)
within the Office of the Associate Attorney General has been engaged in
an all-out
[lawfare](https://www.reuters.com/legal/us-government-sues-illinois-chicago-impeding-immigration-efforts-2025-02-06/)
campaign against cities, counties, and states suspected of being
insufficiently cooperative.

And on February 19th, Trump signed yet another [executive
order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/02/ending-taxpayer-subsidization-of-open-borders/)
cutting off federal funding for such jurisdictions, so that "federal
payments to States and localities do not, by design or effect, abet
so-called 'sanctuary' policies."

**20. Manipulate the truth.**

All the while, the deportation machine's defenders have been seriously
manipulating the truth.

First, ICE has turned images of inmates in captivity into a televised
spectacle, with federal agents bringing [film
crews](https://www.cnn.com/2025/01/27/politics/immigration-raids-federal-agents-uniform/index.html)
and [TV
celebrities](https://people.com/dr-phil-chicago-immigration-raid-8783953)
with them for ride-alongs, even as they [covered
up](https://www.9news.com/article/news/local/ice-agents-cover-ring-security-camera-denver-raids/73-67d20d09-e352-48c5-a29a-56d6a93dd7e9https:/www.9news.com/article/news/local/ice-agents-cover-ring-security-camera-denver-raids/73-67d20d09-e352-48c5-a29a-56d6a93dd7e9)
evidence of their more controversial tactics.

Second, the agency has attempted to make itself look better by
[rewriting
history](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2025/feb/06/ice-us-immigration-deportations-google)
and gaming the Google algorithm by
[manipulating](https://www.dailykos.com/stories/2025/2/6/2301877/-How-ICE-might-be-manipulating-Google-search-results-to-stoke-fear)
the timestamps on thousands of press releases from the first Trump
administration.

Finally, ICE has
[scrubbed](https://tracreports.org/whatsnew/email.250218.html) all
mention of the foreign nationals held in Guantanamo from its public
communications. For days on end, 177 detainees effectively
[disappeared](https://www.salon.com/2025/02/19/intentional-cruelty-administration-accused-of-disappearing-at-guantnamo/).

This is the fate envisioned by the architects of the deportation machine
for America's "tired," its "poor," its "huddled masses yearning to
breathe free."

In the end, however, such an apparatus has a potentially fatal flaw. In
order to function effectively, millions and millions of people must be
willing to go along with it.

The moment too many Americans cease to cooperate, that machinery will
begin to break down in a serious fashion.
