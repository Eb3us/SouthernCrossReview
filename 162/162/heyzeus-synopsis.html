HeyZeus

A Modern Mystery Drama

by Frank Thomas Smith

Synopsis

HeyZeus appears suddenly to Thomas Paulson walking on the lake in – of all places – Prospect Park in Brooklyn. Thomas knows of only one other person who walked on water, but that was a long time ago. He knows that when HeyZeus is pronounced, it sounds like Jesús in Spanish, so he wonders if this HeyZeus, who is Black by the way, could represent the long-awaited Second Coming. At first he is convinced that he does. But when no one else – his friends, the advertising company he works for, his girlfriend, a shrink, a bishop, a theater audience – believes him, he begins to become a doubting Thomas.

But when Magdalena, a pretty, pretended punk who knows HeyZeus and calls him Hey for short, joins Thomas in the Third Act, as does an ICE Agent, who suspects HeyZeus of being an undocumented migrant, a disappointing climax seems inevitable.

But HeyZeus is not merely a guy from Brooklyn who can walk on water and has a sense of humor. He is much more. But what? The prime subject of the second coming? Or, if not, an emissary of the original* Jesús* coming with a message for us all? Once revealed – and practiced – the climax is no longer disappointing, but is, perhaps, more important than we think.
