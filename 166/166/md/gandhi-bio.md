Gandhi](https://www.google.com/search?client=firefox-b-d&cs=0&sca_esv=7fc613d9cd9ef286&sxsrf=AE3TifN75qylegjSXd6Foy8RooqfRA9cNg%3A1756849480064&q=Mahatma+Gandhi&sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwjc15GphruPAxWHKLkGHYMgB7gQxccNegQIAxAB&mstk=AUtExfDhcmOgKkd-ou0p2XEyC0PTcEpEPXt73x0sGxP24WWo4y48Mwz2-k9KMRQzRQa3taooLlv1sMTVkH1-Ii0UrZ8SShEuT9tpNDScFvtiVf-yqCMcAAqoJFGe3M9PGCSMKsII1RA9BwZVx_I0gVNwgZv2wguR1VIcusA6eU8GDRMW-lqNFvuvE77BtI8Lb2GARfF80C5Ncm9CzOlEodtTkgHB3M5dUf7Z21dj2SpnHotAz-HeTZlQ_oPfAWyjM9Ga_w_4c73YzpeyD2L45tb_eqJa&csui=3)
or Bāpū (Father), was an Indian lawyer, anti-colonial activist, and
political ethicist who led the nonviolent Indian Independence Movement
against British rule. Employing his philosophy of nonviolent resistance,
or
[satyagraha](https://www.google.com/search?client=firefox-b-d&cs=0&sca_esv=7fc613d9cd9ef286&sxsrf=AE3TifN75qylegjSXd6Foy8RooqfRA9cNg%3A1756849480064&q=satyagraha&sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwjc15GphruPAxWHKLkGHYMgB7gQxccNegQIBRAB&mstk=AUtExfDhcmOgKkd-ou0p2XEyC0PTcEpEPXt73x0sGxP24WWo4y48Mwz2-k9KMRQzRQa3taooLlv1sMTVkH1-Ii0UrZ8SShEuT9tpNDScFvtiVf-yqCMcAAqoJFGe3M9PGCSMKsII1RA9BwZVx_I0gVNwgZv2wguR1VIcusA6eU8GDRMW-lqNFvuvE77BtI8Lb2GARfF80C5Ncm9CzOlEodtTkgHB3M5dUf7Z21dj2SpnHotAz-HeTZlQ_oPfAWyjM9Ga_w_4c73YzpeyD2L45tb_eqJa&csui=3),
Gandhi inspired civil rights and freedom movements globally. Born in
Porbandar, India, he became a lawyer and social activist, ultimately
becoming the universally recognized leader of India\'s independence.
